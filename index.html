    Have Questions?

    Everything you need to know about OpenHealth HUD

    Is this website a replacement for certified doctors?

    No. OpenHealth is built as an intelligent assistant to offer quick analytical insights based on symptom models. Always consult a licensed healthcare professional for official diagnosis and treatment.

    How does disease prediction work?

    Our backend uses a trained Random Forest Machine Learning model to evaluate vector overlaps against 41 common medical conditions.

    What information is generated in the report?

    You receive the predicted condition, description, preventive action items, medical specialist recommendation, and dietary guidelines.
